f127-load

Build and simulate Pluronic F127 micelles loaded with a hydrophobic solute, at all-atom resolution, with GROMACS and CHARMM36/CGenFF.

Accompanies Entry of a hydrophobic solute into the core interior of Pluronic F127 micelles is slow: an all-atom molecular dynamics study.

What it gives you

  • an equilibrated 34-chain F127 micelle, 200 ns, CHARMM36 with TIP3P, ready to load
  • a hollow-shell template for enclosing a solute as the micelle closes around it
  • a densification step that reaches any polymer concentration without chain overlaps
  • an analysis pipeline covering radial density, uptake, RDF, interaction energy, hydration number, SASA, radius of gyration and solute–solute association
  • a library of molecules commonly loaded into F127

Two loading routes

Simulations of drug-loaded micelles almost always start with the drug already inside the core, so the answer is decided by that choice. This tool builds both ends of the coordinate so that the two can be compared.

Shell closure A hollow poly(propylene oxide) shell is closed around solute placed at the centre. The solute starts as deep as it can go.
Uptake from solution The solute is dispersed in bulk water and enters unaided. This is the situation drug loading actually presents.

Quick start

git clone https://github.com/erjeon/f127-load.git
cd f127-load
bash 0_check.sh                 # GROMACS, python, force field files

# put a CGenFF topology in library/<name>/ first, then
python3 -m f127load.check_params library/pyrene    # penalties
python3 -m f127load.capacity     library/pyrene    # how many will fit
bash scripts/2_load.sh  pyrene 100 solution
bash scripts/3_equilibrate.sh
bash scripts/4_production.sh
bash scripts/5_analyze.sh

Molecule library

Each entry carries an isomeric SMILES, a 3D structure and, once you have run CHARMM-GUI, the CGenFF topology. Stereochemistry is verified against PubChem or the RCSB chemical dictionary rather than typed from memory.

moleculewhy it is here
pyrenethe polarity probe the accompanying paper uses
doxorubicin, paclitaxelthe drugs most often loaded into Pluronics
aspirinsmall and weakly hydrophobic, a lower bound

nile red, docetaxel, quercetin, resveratrolfurther test cases

What it does not do

It does not generate CGenFF parameters. Use CHARMM-GUI Ligand Reader & Modeler and drop the downloaded topology into library/<name>/.

Check the penalties before you trust a result. check_params reports the CGenFF charge and parameter penalties. For a question about where a molecule sits, the charge penalty is what matters, because partitioning between a dry core and water is driven by electrostatics. A high dihedral penalty on a rigid fused ring is usually harmless. Above about 50 on charge, re-parameterise.

Reproducing the paper

The equilibrated micelle, the shell template, the mdp files and the analysis scripts are all here. The trajectories themselves are large and are archived separately.
